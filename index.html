The U.S. food services sector employs approximately 8.6 million Americans at 572,464 small food service establishments, representing 99.6% of all operators (U.S. Small Business Administration Office of Advocacy, 2025), and the traditional restaurant segment generated over $1.1 trillion in annual sales in 2025 (National Restaurant Association, 2025 State of the Restaurant Industry report). Owners and managers spend an estimated 15 or more hours per week on manual data entry. All figures are the working paper's (§1.1, §1.2); none was independently re-verified in this repository.
