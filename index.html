An AI-Driven Financial Intelligence Framework for U.S. SME Restaurant Resilience · Phase-1 proof of concept

In the U.S. Accommodation and Food Services sector, 572,464 small employer firms — 99.6% of all employer firms in the sector — employ more than 8.6 million workers (U.S. Small Business Administration Office of Advocacy, 2025 Small Business Profile: United States). The working paper estimates that owners and managers spend 15 or more hours per week on manual data entry. Figures are as cited in the working paper (§1.1, §1.2); none was independently re-verified in this repository.
